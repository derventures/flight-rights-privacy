# Privacy Policy for Flight Rights Tracker

**Last Updated: May 11, 2026**

## Overview

Flight Rights Tracker ("we," "our," or "the app") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your information.

## Information We Collect

### Information You Provide
- **Email Address** (optional): When you choose to subscribe for updates, we collect your email address to send you app updates, flight rights tips, and feature announcements.
- - **Flight Information**: Flight numbers, airports, and dates you enter to track your flights.
  - - **Profile Information** (optional): Your name, preferred airline, and loyalty program numbers if you choose to save them.
    - - **Notification Preferences**: Your choices about which types of push notifications to receive (Pro subscribers only).
     
      - ### Automatically Collected Information
      - - **Device Information**: We do not collect device identifiers or usage analytics.
        - - **Location Data**: We do not collect or track your location.
          - - **Device Token**: If you are a Pro subscriber and enable push notifications, Apple assigns a device token used solely to deliver local notifications on your device. This token is stored locally and is not sent to our servers.
           
            - ## How We Use Your Information
           
            - We use the information we collect to:
            - - Store and display your tracked flights within the app
              - - Run our decision engine to analyze your flight status and recommend actions (all processing happens locally on your device)
                - - Send local push notifications about decision changes and benefit thresholds (Pro subscribers only, with your permission)
                  - - Send optional email updates about new features and flight rights information (only if you subscribe)
                    - - Manage your subscription status through RevenueCat
                      - - Improve app functionality and user experience
                       
                        - ## Data Storage
                       
                        - - **Flight Data**: Stored locally on your device using secure storage (AsyncStorage). This includes flight snapshots used by our decision engine to detect changes between polling cycles. This data never leaves your device.
                          - - **Decision Engine Data**: Flight state snapshots, decision history, and notification cooldown timestamps are stored locally on your device. No decision data is transmitted externally.
                            - - **Notification Preferences**: Stored locally on your device.
                              - - **Email Addresses**: Stored securely in an encrypted database on Railway.app servers located in the United States.
                               
                                - ## Data Sharing
                               
                                - We do not sell, trade, or share your personal information with third parties, except:
                                - - **AviationStack API**: When you search for a flight, we send the flight number and date to AviationStack's API to retrieve real-time flight data. AviationStack's privacy policy applies to this data transmission.
                                  - - **RevenueCat**: We use RevenueCat to manage in-app subscriptions. RevenueCat receives an anonymous app user ID and purchase receipts from Apple to verify your subscription status. RevenueCat does not receive your name, email, or flight data. See [RevenueCat's privacy policy](https://www.revenuecat.com/privacy/).
                                   
                                    - We will never share your email address with third parties for marketing purposes.
                                   
                                    - ## Your Rights
                                   
                                    - You have the right to:
                                    - - **Access**: View what data we have stored about you
                                      - - **Delete**: Request deletion of your email from our database at any time
                                        - - **Opt-Out**: Unsubscribe from emails at any time
                                          - - **Disable Notifications**: Turn off push notifications at any time through the app's Profile screen or your device's Settings
                                           
                                            - To exercise these rights, contact us at: rosenzweig.derek@gmail.com
                                           
                                            - ## Data Retention
                                           
                                            - - **Flight Data**: Stored on your device until you delete the app or manually remove flights
                                              - - **Decision Engine Data**: Stored on your device until you delete the app
                                                - - **Email Addresses**: Retained until you request deletion
                                                 
                                                  - ## Children's Privacy
                                                 
                                                  - Flight Rights Tracker is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect information from children under 13.
                                                 
                                                  - ## Security
                                                 
                                                  - We implement industry-standard security measures to protect your data:
                                                  - - HTTPS encryption for all network communications
                                                    - - Encrypted database storage for email addresses
                                                      - - No storage of sensitive personal or financial information
                                                        - - All flight analysis and decision processing occurs locally on your device
                                                         
                                                          - ## Third-Party Services

                                                          - - **AviationStack**: Flight data API (https://aviationstack.com/privacy)

                                                              - - **RevenueCat**: Subscription management (https://www.revenuecat.com/privacy/)
                                                                - - **Apple Push Notification service (APNs)**: Used to deliver local notifications on your device (Pro subscribers only)
