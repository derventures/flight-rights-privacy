# FlightRights Data Deletion Request

Last Updated: June 25, 2026

FlightRights users may request deletion of their personal data at any time.

## How to Request Deletion

Please email:

**derek@der-ventures.com**

with the subject line:

**Data Deletion Request**

Please include the email address associated with your FlightRights communications or subscription.

## Data That Will Be Deleted

Upon request, we will delete:

- Email addresses stored for app updates and communications
- Communication preferences associated with your email address

## Data Stored on Your Device

Flight information, saved preferences, and decision history are stored locally on your device. You may delete this information at any time by deleting the FlightRights app from your device.

## Subscription Information

Subscription purchases are managed through Apple App Store, Google Play, and RevenueCat. Certain transaction records may be retained by Apple, Google, or RevenueCat as required for billing, accounting, fraud prevention, or legal compliance.

## Retention Period

Deletion requests are typically completed within 30 days.

## Questions

If you have questions regarding data deletion, please contact:

**derek@der-ventures.com**
